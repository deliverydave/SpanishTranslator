DeliveryDave jobsite SMS privacy

This page covers the bilingual SMS bridge used for DeliveryDave jobsite and operations texts. It is not a marketing list and it is not used for stocks, tickers, catalysts, or investment alerts.

What we use

  • The Twilio number that sits between the owner and one jobsite contact
  • Phone numbers of that configured pair
  • Message text, so it can be translated and forwarded
  • Opt-out records after you text STOP

Why

So a Spanish-speaking contact and an English-speaking owner can text about the job (schedule, materials, deliveries, site notes) each in their own language.

Who else sees a message

  • Twilio delivers the SMS
  • xAI (Grok) translates the text

We do not sell this data. We do not use it for market or investment messages.

Your choices

Text STOP to opt out (that word stays English). Text START to rejoin if you are the configured owner or contact. Text HELP for help. Email contact@deliverydave.ai.
