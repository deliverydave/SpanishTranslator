DeliveryDave jobsite SMS terms

These texts are for DeliveryDave jobsite and operations use only (schedules, materials, deliveries, invoices, field coordination). They are not emergency services and they are not stock, ticker, catalyst, ACap, or investment messages.

How it works

A Twilio number sits between the owner and one configured contact. Each inbound text is translated and forwarded to the other person, with a short DeliveryDave notice.

Frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with the job. Message and data rates may apply.

Keywords (English)

  • STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT — opt out. We confirm and do not forward that text.
  • HELP or INFO — help, plus contact@deliverydave.ai and these pages.
  • START, YES, or UNSTOP — rejoin only if your number is the configured owner or contact.

Translation

Machine translation can be imperfect. Confirm times, addresses, quantities, and money in person or with a follow-up if a detail matters on site.
